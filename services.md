# Services

## What I Do

### IAM Advisory
Strategic guidance for organizations at any stage of their identity
journey — whether you're building an IAM program from scratch, evaluating
vendors, or course-correcting an implementation that's gone off track.

### Identity Governance & Administration (IGA) Implementation
End-to-end implementation of SailPoint Identity Security Cloud (ISC) - 
from initial scoping through go-live and ongoing optimization.

### IAM Strategy & Roadmapping
Assessment, gap analysis, and strategic roadmap development tailored to
your organization's identity maturity and business goals.

### Access Governance & Compliance
Role mining, access reviews, separation of duties (SoD) policies, and
compliance-aligned access certification programs.

### Federation & SSO
SAML, OIDC, and OAuth 2.0 implementation across enterprise applications
and SaaS integrations.

### Lifecycle Management
Joiner/Mover/Leaver automation, SCIM provisioning, and self-service
access workflows.

### Zero Trust Architecture
Identity-centric security design — conditional access, continuous
verification, and least-privilege access models.
