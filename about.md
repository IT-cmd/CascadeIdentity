## Daniel Roberts

I'm an identity and access management consultant based in Vancouver, 
British Columbia. I've spent my career helping organizations secure their digital
identities — from large-scale identity provider migrations to access
governance programs and zero trust architecture design.

### Expertise
- SailPoint Identity Security Cloud (ISC)
- Microsoft Entra ID / Azure AD
- SCIM & lifecycle management
- IAM SME Advisory
- JML Automation
- Role-based & attribute-based access control
- Zero trust architecture
- Access governance & compliance
